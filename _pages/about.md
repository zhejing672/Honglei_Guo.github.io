I am a first-year Ph.D. student jointly trained by the [College of Artificial Intelligence, Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) and [BIGAI](https://bigai.ai/) (Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence). My academic advisor is Prof. Guochuan Zhang, and my BIGAI mentor is Yuhan Zhao in the Social Intelligence Department. Prior to my doctoral studies, I received my bachelor's degree from [China University of Geosciences, Beijing](https://en.cugb.edu.cn/), advised by Ling Bu.

## Research Interests

- **Time Series Forecasting:** Spatio-temporal forecasting and photovoltaic power forecasting.
- **Game Theory and Multi-Agent Systems:** Multi-agent reinforcement learning for economic and social systems.
- **Embodied AI:** Multi-robot systems, including MARL-based and LLM-based approaches.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration and thoughtful discussion.
