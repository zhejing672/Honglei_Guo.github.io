I am Honglei Guo, a researcher affiliated with Zhejiang University and BIGAI (Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence). My research interests lie in time series forecasting, game theory, multi-agent systems, and embodied AI, with a focus on developing principled and efficient methods for intelligent systems that learn, reason, coordinate, and act in complex real-world environments.

My recent work spans multi-agent reinforcement learning for economic and social systems, spatio-temporal and energy forecasting, and embodied intelligence. My publications appear at venues such as ICLR and IROS, as well as leading journals including *Applied Physics Letters*, *International Journal of Digital Earth*, and *Energy Conversion and Management: X*.
